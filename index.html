07How we work

A clear pathway from assessment to commissioning

We follow a simple, risk-free process to ensure your system generates maximum power from day one.

  1. .01

    Feasibility & Consumption Audit

    We analyse your premises, sun exposure, electricity grid reliability, and historical records of your electricity bills to determine actual cost savings.

  2. .02

    Custom Engineering

    We create custom engineering designs according to the specifics of your premises and energy demands.

  3. .03

    Hardware Sourcing

    We provide top-notch hardware capable of working under extreme weather conditions, meeting your site’s operating conditions, and offering warranty coverage.

  4. .04

    On-Site Construction

    All installation processes, such as civil works, mounting, and wiring, are carried out under our engineers’ supervision.

  5. .05

    Grid Connection & Testing

    We test safety systems, confirm real-time power output, and sync the setup directly into your facility or the main grid.
