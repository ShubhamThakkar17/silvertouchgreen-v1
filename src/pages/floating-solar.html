01Why floating solar

The problem of limited land and expensive land prices restricts the scope of expanding solar power systems in commercial buildings, utility services, and industries.

The Floating Solar (or Floating Photovoltaic/FPV) technology tackles this issue through the installation of solar panels on appropriate water bodies. This converts underutilised aquatic space into efficient clean energy generators.

Silver Touch Green RE provides floating solar solutions that increase power generation while sparing the use of scarce land resources.

The natural cooling of solar panels due to water improves their performance and increases power generation by up to 10 to 15%.

02Key operational advantages

Clear advantages in structure, cost and environment

The floating solar systems offer clear advantages in terms of structure, costs, and environment for industrial and civil facilities.

Zero land footprint

Generate clean electricity on existing reservoirs, holding ponds, quarry lakes, and industrial water bodies without taking up real estate.

Higher panel yields

Water below the panels reduces the temperature, preventing any drop in efficiency levels to optimise kilowatt-hours during peak heat.

Reduced water evaporation & algae growth

Module coverage shades the water surface, significantly cutting evaporative water loss and suppressing harmful algae blooms.

03Who benefits

Who benefits from floating solar?

Industrial water & utility bodies

Municipal reservoirs, water treatment plants, and hydroelectric facilities looking to generate co-located renewable power.

Mining & heavy processing plants

Facilities with large retention ponds, tailing dams, or artificial quarry lakes.

Agricultural & commercial estates

Large-scale irrigation networks and commercial facilities with unused water storage bodies.
