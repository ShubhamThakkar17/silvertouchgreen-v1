Are you experiencing unplanned power outages, voltage sags, and volatile peak-hour electricity tariffs?

Risk 01

Unplanned power outages

Risk 02

Risk 03

Volatile peak-hour tariffs

Demand charges climb when your consumption peaks at the times the grid charges most.

While these are common, they pose direct risks to operational continuity and business profitability. The solution? Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS).

If you are seeking to protect operations and optimise power costs, you've come to the right place.

At Silver Touch Green RE, we design and deploy enterprise-grade Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) that turn power management from a passive overhead into an active, controlled operational asset. By capturing electricity when grid rates are lowest or when on-site solar generation peaks, our battery solutions allow industrial plants, commercial properties, and utilities to discharge power precisely when costs spike or grid supply drops out.

03How we deliver

End-to-end engineering & integration framework

Utilisation of high-capacity battery storage is contingent upon thorough electrical engineering for safety and speed of disconnection from the grid, along with maintaining temperature stability. We provide lifecycle management from start to finish.

Interval load profiling01 / 05
01Step

Interval load profiling

Our engineers analyse your site's historical electrical data, kW peak demand surges, and critical backup requirements to specify exact power (kW) and energy (kWh) capacity.

02Step

Safety architecture & layout

We design bespoke containerised setups equipped with advanced Battery Management Systems (BMS), thermal controls, and multi-tier fire suppression.

03Step

High-grade cell sourcing

Our choice of battery chemistry is selected based on its quality as well as suitability for high cycle life, thermal stability, and warranty support.

04Step

Supervised on-site installation

Civil foundations, container placement, and high-voltage wiring are executed under strict safety protocols and direct engineering oversight.

05Step

EMS calibration & system syncing

We program local Energy Management System (EMS) software to automate peak shaving routines and test emergency backup switchover speeds prior to commissioning.

04Partner with Silver Touch Green RE

Rather than supplying generic, off-the-shelf battery cabinets, we engineer custom storage systems designed specifically around your facility's load profile.

From initial load profiling and safety compliance to control software calibration, Silver Touch Green RE delivers the technical expertise needed to secure your plant's energy resilience.
